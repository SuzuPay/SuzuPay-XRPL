### description

**Web3 Salon and Asia Web3 Alliance Japan** , in collaboration with the **Japan External Trade Organization (JETRO)** and **Ripple** , announced the launch of the **Japan Financial Infrastructure Innovation Program (JFIIP)** .

The program empowers Japanese startups to build **regulated, enterprise-grade digital finance solutions on the XRP Ledger (XRPL)** , reinforcing Japan’s leadership in compliant blockchain innovation and reflecting Ripple’s commitment to supporting developers through technical, business, and funding support.

This national acceleration initiative is designed to empower next-generation Web3 and FinTech startups , positioning Japan as a global leader in regulated digital finance and financial infrastructure innovation .

### Program Vision

**The Japan Financial Infrastructure Innovation Program (JFIIP)** is a **venture studio–led initiative** that bridges **enterprise technology, financial institutions, and government-supported ecosystems** to provide startups with **hands-on support** in building **commercially viable, regulation-aligned financial infrastructure** for both the Japanese and global markets.

Through this venture studio model, the program is designed to:

- Support startups with **end-to-end venture building** , from concept development to market-ready solutions

- Enable **direct collaboration with institutional partners** , including banks, security firms, and enterprise technology leaders

- Provide access to **regulatory insight and policy-aligned guidance** , supporting responsible innovation

- Offer **hands-on mentorship, technical support, and business development assistance**


- Deliver **funding incentives and awards** to accelerate product development and reduce early-stage risk

- Prepare startups for **enterprise pilots, institutional adoption, and long term scaling**

- Strengthen Japan's position as a **global hub for digital finance and financial infrastructure innovation**

- Create sustainable growth by fostering **scalable, compliant, and enterprise-ready ventures**


### Incentives & Awards

The **Japan Financial Infrastructure Innovation Program (JFIIP)** offers structured incentives to support startups in accelerating product development and preparing for commercial deployment.

Selected teams will complete the program with a **working prototype** and gain access to **funding rewards and scale-up opportunities** designed to support continued growth.

- During Phase 2, selected startups enter an **intensive venture studio support phase** focused on helping founders and their teams **bring a live, working product to market before Demo Day**.

Startups receive **hands-on guidance** through a combination of **online sessions and in-person interactions**, working closely with **institutional partners, industry experts, and mentors**. Support spans product development, technical architecture, compliance awareness, business strategy, and go-to-market execution.

Through direct collaboration with institutional partners, startups refine real-world use cases, validate enterprise requirements, and prepare their solutions for commercial deployment. The program ensures founders and teams are supported not only at the idea level, but through **practical execution and delivery**.

**Outcome:**

By the end of Phase 2, each selected startup is expected to present a **live, production-ready prototype** that demonstrates commercial viability and readiness for institutional engagement at Demo Day.


**Date:** February 24, 2026

**Location:** JETRO Tokyo Headquarters

##### Demo Day, Awards Ceremony & Industry Roundtable

**Event Status:** Official Certified Program Event of **Japan FinTech Week**

Phase 3 marks the culmination of the **Japan Financial Infrastructure Innovation Program (JFIIP)**. Co-hosted by **JETRO**, the Demo Day will bring together **policymakers, financial institutions, corporates, investors, and startup founders**.

Selected startups will present their **enterprise-ready solutions** developed during the program, followed by:

- **Awards Ceremony** recognizing excellence in innovation, scalability, and regulatory alignment

- **Closed-door Industry Roundtable** focused on commercial deployment strategies, regulatory insights, and pathways for institutional adoption


This final phase provides startups with a high-level platform to engage directly with key decision-makers and advance toward real-world implementation.
